## Good to know

- New v6 scoring: add `--out_root results/v6` to `python -m src.evaluate`, otherwise the result lands in `results/`.
- New v6 training: give the experiment name as `v6/<name>` to put the model under `checkpoints/v6/` (v7 does the same with `v7/<name>`; not tested for v6).
- New v6 training still writes its pictures to `visu/epoch_*.png`, outside `visu/v6/`: that folder name is fixed inside `src/train.py`,
  which was not changed. Move them afterwards into a folder of their own, so that no old picture is overwritten:
  `mkdir visu/v6/<run name> && mv visu/epoch_*.png visu/v6/<run name>/`.
- On the laptop nothing moves by itself except the results (through git). The laptop's own data and models stay where they are.
