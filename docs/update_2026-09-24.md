# Update for the meeting, 24 September 2026

## One-line summary

A small model reading Google AlphaEarth embeddings reproduces the ESA CCI biomass map to within CCI's own stated per-pixel uncertainty (RMSE ≈ 0.73 × CCI SD), in France (trained) and in Sweden (never trained on).

## Results

| | RMSE (Mg/ha) | R² | vs CCI SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridge, linear per pixel, France test | 31.7 | 0.73 | |
| PointWise MLP, France test | 27.7 | 0.80 | |
| SmallCNN, France test | 26.3 | 0.82 | 0.73 |
| SmallCNN, Sweden (transfer, 100 tiles) | 27.0 | 0.65 | 0.72 |

- Linear already gets R² 0.73: the information is in the embedding.
- Stable across two independent random samples of France (26.1 vs 26.5 RMSE).
- **Seed repeats (3 CNN runs, same test tiles, different weight init), done overnight:**

  | | RMSE | R² | bias | RMSE / CCI SD |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | France test | 26.7 ± 0.6 | 0.81 ± 0.01 | +0.8 ± 1.3 | 0.74 ± 0.02 |
  | Sweden | 24.4 ± 3.5 | 0.71 ± 0.08 | +6.1 ± 6.5 | 0.65 ± 0.09 |

  In-domain is tight. Out-of-domain the spread is large: two seeds over-predict Sweden by ~10 Mg/ha, one is unbiased (RMSE 20.4, R² 0.80). Transfer quality depends on the particular solution the training found, so any transfer claim needs several seeds.

- **Ensemble of the three (predictions averaged), done 24 Sep morning:**

  | | RMSE | R² | bias | RMSE / CCI SD | within 1 SD |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | France test | 26.1 | 0.82 | +0.8 | 0.73 | 67 % |
  | Sweden | **21.9** | **0.77** | +6.1 | **0.58** | 80 % |

  Averaging cancels the random part of the transfer error (Sweden RMSE 24.4 → 21.9, R² 0.71 → 0.77) but not the bias, which is simply the members' mean (+6). In-domain nothing changes, as expected. The ensemble is now the best Sweden result and the recommended model for transfer.
- Saturation above ~150 Mg/ha, inherited from the reference.

   Same shape in both countries: over-prediction below ~50, under-prediction above ~150 (regression to the mean plus the reference's own saturation). France's near-zero overall bias is a cancellation of the two ends. Sweden has almost no pixels above 150 to cancel with, and a third of its pixels sit in 50–100, where the model over-reads Swedish (boreal conifer) forest by ~10 vs −4 for French forest of the same density. So: mostly a composition effect, plus a genuine shift in the 50–100 band. Fix candidates: include a boreal region in training, or calibrate per biomass band.

## Likely questions, one-line answers

- **Why is your RMSE lower than the published ones?** Ours is map-to-map (target = CCI). Published numbers are against field plots or GEDI. The right comparison is Sialelli 2026: an AlphaEarth model on par with CCI against plots. Same conclusion from the other side.
- **Isn't the model just learning CCI's errors?** Yes, partly, and that is the point: the question is whether the embedding *contains* what CCI encodes. Error ≈ 0.6–0.7 × CCI's own SD says it contains nearly all of it. A plot-based evaluation (AGBD Lite / AGBref) is the follow-up that answers the stronger question.
- **Why does the linear model already work so well?** AlphaEarth is trained so that linear probes work (that is its design goal). R² 0.73 linear vs 0.82 CNN means most of the signal is linearly readable; the CNN adds non-linearity and a little spatial context.
- **Why the bias in Sweden?** See the table above: same error pattern as France, different pixel mix, plus an over-read of boreal forest in the 50–100 band. Ensemble reduces the random part; the systematic part needs boreal training data or per-band calibration.
- **What about high biomass?** Under-predicted above ~150 Mg/ha in both regions, like every SAR-based map (Avitabile & Camia 2018 find the same for all European maps). It is inherited from the target as much as from the model.
- **Only 100 tiles?** Each tile is 65k pixels; 70 training tiles ≈ 4.6 M labelled pixels. Two independent 100-tile samples of France gave the same numbers (26.1 vs 26.5), so the sample size is not the limit.
- **Which ECV next?** Anything ≤ 1 km native: land cover (300 m), burned area (250 m), snow. Coarse ECVs (SST, soil moisture, 0.25°) need a different tile design. The toolbox path is ready for either fine one; the ODP store bug means v4 for biomass but should not affect other ECVs (to verify).
- **Could this replace the CCI product?** No; it reproduces it. The interesting use is the reverse: embeddings as a cheap, annually updated proxy where the full retrieval is expensive, and as a consistency check between ECVs.
